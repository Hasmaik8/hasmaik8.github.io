Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information SPY Wall ("we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. SPY Wall is a private, invitation-only dashboard; access is given only to people the operator invites.

1. Information we collect

  • Login name. The username you were given to sign in to the dashboard.
  • Linked brokerage data (only if you choose to link a broker). Brokerage connections are made through SnapTrade, a third-party service. The link is read-only. Through it we receive the list of your brokerage accounts and your recently executed orders (such as the symbol, side, quantity, price and time of a fill).
  • Technical information. Our servers keep ordinary operating logs (for example, the time of a request and the page requested) to run, secure and fix the service.

What we never receive: your brokerage username or password. You sign in to your broker on SnapTrade's or your broker's own page, never on ours. We do not collect payment card details, and the link cannot place, change or cancel orders or move money.

2. How we use it

  • To show you, and only you, how your own trades compare with the dashboard's alerts.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Each user sees only their own brokerage information. It is not shown to other users.

3. How we share it

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only:

  • with SnapTrade, which provides the brokerage connection under its own privacy policy;
  • with the infrastructure providers that host and deliver the service (cloud server and secure tunnel providers), only as needed to run it;
  • if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the service.

4. Storage and security

Information is stored on servers in the United States. Access to the dashboard requires a login, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and the files that hold brokerage-link data are readable only by the service itself. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect your broker at any time from the dashboard. Disconnecting deletes your SnapTrade user (which removes the brokerage connection) and our stored link details. You may also email hasmaik8@comcast.net to ask for a copy of your information or for its deletion, and we will act on the request within 30 days. Records we must keep by law, or ordinary server logs that age out on their own, may be kept for a limited time.

6. Children

SPY Wall is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to object to how it is used. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated to users through the dashboard or by email.
