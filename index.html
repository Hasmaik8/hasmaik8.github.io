Signal Desk

Market alerts and trade tracking for U.S. exchange-traded funds — see the signal, then see how your own trades compared.

What Signal Desk does

  • Market alerts. Signal Desk watches several of the largest U.S. exchange-traded funds (such as SPY, QQQ and IWM) and shows clear alerts when its rules see a setup, with the reasoning in plain English.
  • Practice results. Every alert is tracked with practice (paper) trades, so you can see how the alerts have actually done over days, weeks and months.
  • Your own trades, side by side. If you choose, you can link your brokerage account read-only. Signal Desk then shows which alerts you acted on and how your trades compared.
  • Works on your phone. The dashboard is built for a phone screen first.

How the broker link works

Broker connections are provided by SnapTrade. You sign in to your broker on SnapTrade's or your broker's own secure page — Signal Desk never sees your broker username or password. The link is read-only: Signal Desk can read your accounts and recent executed orders, but it cannot place, change or cancel trades or move money. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the link. Your brokerage information is shown only to you.

Who can use it

Signal Desk is in a small early-access stage. Access is by invitation to adults in the United States. To ask for access, email hasmaik8@comcast.net.

Important: Signal Desk is for information and education only. It is not investment advice, does not place trades for you, and practice results are hypothetical. Trading involves risk of loss.
