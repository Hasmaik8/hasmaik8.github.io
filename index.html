SPY Wall

SPY Wall is a private, invitation-only trading dashboard. It shows market alerts and practice (paper) trading results for several U.S. exchange-traded funds, and lets each invited user optionally link a brokerage account, read-only, so the dashboard can show how their own trades compare with the alerts.

SPY Wall does not place trades for users and does not give personal investment advice.
